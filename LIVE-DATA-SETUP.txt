ONE-TIME DATABASE UPDATE

Open Supabase Dashboard > SQL Editor.
Paste and run:
supabase/ENTERPRISE-B2B-LIVE-DATA.sql

This creates or extends the live B2B tables used by Dashboard, Won leads, Companies, Contacts, Approvals, Follow-Ups, Certifications, Forms, Samples, Shipments, Activity Logs, FAQ Intelligence, GEO, Reports and AI Agents.

The SQL file does not insert fake leads, clients, quotations or shipments.
